Three minor findings below; no critical or major.

**1. `rule_inproc.py:87` — the stage-2 subject guard is effectively a 3-word whitelist, and the target it emits still carries counting-noise.** Stage 2 only runs when stage 1 emptied, i.e. when *every* word is in `_SCAN_NOISE`, so `w not in _SCAN_NOISE` in the guard can never be True — the real gate is `w in ("color","colour","number")`. And since stage 2 drops only grammar words, the noise stays: I probed `"how many times does the color change in the video?"` → `"the times color change video"`, which is exactly the counting-noise shape round 1 flagged, now with a subject bolted on. Smallest fix that collapses both stages and the dead branch: `[w for w in words if w not in _SCAN_NOISE or w in _SUBJECT_NOUNS]` with `_SUBJECT_NOUNS = {"color","colour","number"}` (the nouns `_STOP` absorbs for search) — that yields `"the color"`, keeps `"how many times did it change?"` → `None`, and makes a future `_STOP` noun addition fail loudly rather than silently deriving `None`.

**2. `ask.py:255` — self-escalation re-reasons over unchanged evidence when the escalated sweep is skipped.** If the first answer is insufficient and the sweep is then vetoed (profile `off`, or no derivable target), `evidence.items` is unmodified but the code still re-collects keyframes and makes a second `reason()` call. On security-profile footage that is the *common* path — every honest abstention costs a duplicated reasoner call (this branch's own evidence measures those at 100–330 s). Safe path: after `_deep_scan_into`, only re-reason if `any(i.modality == "deep_scan_count" for i in evidence.items)`; otherwise keep the first answer and the skip note.

**3. `COORDINATION.md:580` — the ledger entry describes the wrong stage-2 filter.** It says derivation degrades "to the search-stopword filter", but the shipped stage 2 uses `_TARGET_GRAMMAR`, deliberately *not* `_STOP`, because `_STOP` would strip the query's subject nouns — the distinction the round-1 fix turned on. The next agent reading the cross-agent log will model behavior the code doesn't have. Correct the line to say grammar-words-only with the `{color, colour, number}` subject-noun exception.
