## Findings

**minor — `src/va/pipeline/retrieval.py:208`** — `gates_by_video` early-returns before ever calling `footage_domains()` when no candidate carries a `video_id`, so `GateMap.domains` is empty and `retrieve` skips the mixed-domain note in the extreme of the very case the design exists for.

*Failure scenario:* a mixed workdir whose visual shards are on a stale embedder (tag-skipped, so `visual_query` returns `[]`) and whose plan requests no text tier produces zero video-bearing items. The answer comes back with no candidates and no hint that the workdir spans A-EV and A-LSSRVF footage — total burial silencing the warning, which the docstring at `:200-202` and the CLAUDE.md paragraph both promise it will not do. *Safe path:* `domains` is a property of the workdir, not of the pool — query `footage_domains()` unconditionally and short-circuit only the per-video gate lookup (`if not vids: return GateMap(gates, <domains>)`), then extend `test_a_mixed_domain_pool_is_flagged_in_the_evidence` with an empty-pool case.

**minor — `src/va/storage/structured/catalog_sqlite.py:126`** — `footage_domains()` documents itself as covering "SEARCHABLE videos" but filters on `ingest_status = 'done'`, and a video becomes searchable at `store.persist()` (`ingest.py:595`), roughly 110 lines before `set_status(done)` (`ingest.py:704`).

*Failure scenario:* `va ingest "nvr://…"` into a workdir that already holds YouTube videos; the un-guarded `_purge("object_tracker", …)` at `ingest.py:611` raises (or the process is killed / `va serve` restarts mid-job, leaving `processing`). The row lands on `failed`/`processing` while `videos/<key>/vectors.npz` is persisted and enumerated by `ShardedVectorStore` forever after. Those NVR frames then compete in every pool — correctly gated per video, since `get_many` ignores status — but `footage_domains()` reports one domain, so no answer in that workdir ever warns that A-LSSRVF footage is being ranked on an A-EV scale. That is the same silent under-representation the note was added to surface, arriving through the opposite door from the failed-before-fetch case the `done` filter was chosen to block. *Safe path:* key the domain set on shard existence (the videos `ShardedVectorStore` actually enumerates) rather than on status, which excludes the never-fetched row and includes the persisted-but-unfinished one; or, if the `done` filter stays, narrow the docstring from "SEARCHABLE" to "completed" and record the residual hole next to the other R11.b known gaps in `architecture-evolution-loop.md`.
