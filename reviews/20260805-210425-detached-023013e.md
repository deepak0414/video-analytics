Three minor findings survive, none blocking:

1. **Crop-cache leak on the critical path** — `shutil.rmtree(crops_dir)` at `ingest.py:605` is not in a `finally`, so a Role-2 embedding failure (which aborts ingest) or a killed process strands `cache/crops-<video>/` with potentially thousands of JPEGs, and nothing ever prunes `cache/`. A `try/finally` around the decode→track span fixes it.
2. **Appearance store invisible to the staleness machinery** — `_reprocess_visual_embedder` rebuilds only `vectors.npz`; after an embedder upgrade, `appearance.npz` keeps old-model vectors while `va stale` reads clean (the shard meta tag is the only detection hook). Harmless today (nothing searches the store), but WS12.b's gallery matching over mixed-embedder vectors would compute garbage distances — worth a backlog note or a rebuild-or-delete in that reprocess path.
3. **Best-crop selection ignores crop availability** — `_capture_appearance` picks the highest-confidence detection first and only then looks up its crop; a sub-pixel-wide box (distant object on wide-angle NVR footage — the security profile's own domain) int-rounds to zero area at harvest, so the track silently loses its ref even when other frames have usable crops. Selecting the max-confidence detection *among those with a crop key* recovers those tracks.
