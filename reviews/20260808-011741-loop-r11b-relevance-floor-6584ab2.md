## Findings

**minor — `tests/test_relevance_gate_profile.py:238`** — `test_source_types_sharing_a_profile_are_one_domain` ingests exactly one *local* video, so the scenario its name and docstring describe (a local **and** a youtube video collapsing to one `generic` domain) is never constructed. The assertion it does make (`domains == frozenset({"generic"})`) is the same one already made at line 226 of the preceding test.

*Failure scenario:* someone refactors `gates_by_video` to keep `domains` as profile names but derive the mix decision from the raw `(profile, source_type)` pairs — e.g. `if len(workdir_domains) > 1` in `retrieval.py:541` instead of `len(gmap.domains) > 1`. This test still passes (one video, one pair, names unchanged), and `.va-shots` — one local plus five youtube videos, all `generic` — starts appending "workdir spans 2 footage profiles (generic, generic)" to every answer, which is the exact regression the docstring says it guards. Per CLAUDE.md's 2026-07-28 lesson, a regression test that cannot reproduce the failure it names is decoration.

*Safe path:* add a second source type before asserting, using the fabricated-row pattern already in this file (`catalog.upsert(Video(source_type=SourceType.youtube, …, ingest_status=IngestStatus.done))` at line 217), so `footage_domains()` genuinely returns two pairs; then assert both `domains == frozenset({"generic"})` and that `retrieve()` on that workdir emits no "footage profiles" note.

**minor — commit body (`6584ab2`)** — the body still pairs "the A-EV floor retained 6/26 ground-truth clips" with "7/25 -> 13/25 at k=8" without saying they come from different query sets and different denominators — the exact ambiguity this commit fixed in `video-analytics-model-analysis.md`, COORDINATION.md, and all three `run-*/config` profile comments.

*Failure scenario:* the commit body is the permanent history entry a future bisect or blame lands on first. A reader there sees 7-in-final-evidence apparently exceeding the 6 that survived the floor — impossible for a subset — and either distrusts the whole measurement or re-derives the numbers against a different denominator when revisit trigger (a) fires and the embedder is swapped. The provisional `need_agent_review:` *subject* is exempt from the clarity rule, but the body survives the finalize amend.

*Safe path:* at finalize, carry the one-clause disambiguation already written elsewhere into the body — "6/26 (query, clip) pairs across the 9 targeted queries, at the gate" and "7/25 across the 8 end-to-end questions, also bounded by the k-capped gather".
